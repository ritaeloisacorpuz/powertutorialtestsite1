Public Directory of Apprentice Tutors

Do you want discounted tutorial fees? The Public Directory provides a selection of Apprentice Tutors that offer a discounted rate, but unlike the official Power Tutorial tutors featured on this main tutor page, Apprentice Tutors in the Public Directory are not official PT tutors. At least, not yet. Right now, they are alternative tutor options. Apprentice Tutors in the Public Directory are not verified by Power Tutorial Management yet since anyone can sign up in the Public Directory. However, highly qualified Apprentice Tutors who registered in the Public Directory are sometimes eventually invited into being included in the Main Page as a Power Tutor if their qualifications are at par with the standards.

In the long term, though, many Apprentice Tutors eventually become official Power Tutorial tutors after a series of evaluations and based on feedback of Booking Students like yourself. In the meanwhile, the discount allows you to test the tutor's teaching method if it matches your child's learning style before you start paying full price. Just note that these Apprentice Tutors' slightly discounted rate varies per Apprentice Tutor.
