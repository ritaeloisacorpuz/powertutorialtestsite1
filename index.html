Why Choose Us

We are all unique. We all learn differently. We all have different strengths. Power Tutorial recognizes this and customizes its teaching methods to accommodate your child's specific needs to better achieve their learning objectives in the shortest possible time.

Differentiated Learning Methods

Lessons are tailored to meet the diverse needs, interests and learning style of each student, which allows each student to progress at their own pace.

Technology-Enhanced Instruction

Digital tools such as interactive simulations, videos and online collaboration enrich learning and make abstract concepts more accessible.

Constructivist Approach

Students actively build their own understanding through hands-on experience, exploration and reflection with the teacher serving as facilitator.
